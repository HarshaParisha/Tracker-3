# 90-Day Personal Transformation Tracker 🚀

I personally built this tracker to work on myself for 3 months — to stay disciplined, build solid daily habits, and track my personal growth step by step every single day.

---

## 🌅 Why I Built This

I wanted a clean, calm, and distraction-free system to hold myself accountable without feeling overwhelmed. This tracker helps me keep track of:

- 🏃 **Daily Routine & Fitness**: Morning workouts, outdoor walks, and evening temple reflection.
- 💧 **Health & Hydration**: Daily water targets, creatine intake, healthy meals, and mood tracking.
- 💻 **Skill Development & Learning**: Focused DSA problem solving, Python coding sessions, revision, market research, and AI tool exploration.
- 💼 **Career Pipeline**: Daily job application tracking and status updates.
- 📝 **Daily Journaling**: End-of-day reflections, wins, lessons, and tomorrow's prep.
- 🏖️ **Weekend Recovery**: Dedicated Saturday & Sunday routine for family time, networking, outdoor exploration, and Sunday meal preps.

---

## 🔔 Smart Reminders & Sync

It includes automatic scheduled reminders throughout the day to keep me on track, along with cloud sync so I can access it seamlessly across my laptop and phone.
